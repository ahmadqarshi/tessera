About the asset

Prenzlauer Höfe is a fully let 214-unit residential block completed in 1998 and refurbished in 2021. Rents are indexed to the Berlin Mietspiegel and the building has held occupancy above 96% for eleven consecutive quarters. The tranche represents a senior claim on net rental income and on proceeds if the asset is sold.

Title is held by Tessera Berlin Propco I GmbH, a bankruptcy-remote entity whose shares are pledged to a security trustee acting for token holders. Quarterly distributions are paid in EURC to the wallet holding the tranche.
